# VeilAP brand kit

## The idea in one line

Private payment operations should feel like a controlled financial record, not a crypto dashboard.

## Identity

VeilAP pairs the idea of a veil with accounts payable. The canonical mark is a split `V` held inside an ink square. Its vermilion left stroke is the private instruction, its paper right stroke is the disclosed record, and the ink cut between them is the privacy boundary.

The visual wordmark is `VEILAP` as one uppercase word, with `AP` in a heavier weight. The product name remains `VeilAP` in prose. Do not write `VEIL AP` or use `V/` as an alternate mark.

The mark may stand alone. The wordmark must keep clear space equal to half the mark's height. Use the same square mark for the favicon, navigation, workspace, and social assets. Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, round, shadow, or place it on a busy image.

Canonical source asset: `public/brand/veilap-mark.svg`.

## Color

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#191915` | Permanent record, primary type, and disclosure sheet |
| Security paper | `#e9e1d1` | Main workspace and controlled-document field |
| Clean paper | `#f3eddf` | Ledger and authorization surfaces |
| Quiet text | `#5d5b52` | Supporting copy and annotations |
| Rule | `#9e998c` | Dividers, ledger lines, and registration marks |
| Signal | `#ee5736` | Release stamp and approval action only |
| Compliance green | `#264b3a` | Validated controls and ready states |

Signal vermilion is the single product accent. It should feel like an approval stamp, not decorative product chrome.

## Type

- Display: Newsreader Variable for editorial emphasis and document totals.
- Body: Manrope Variable for product copy, headings, navigation, and controls.
- Labels: Manrope Variable in compact uppercase settings for document metadata and navigation.
- Verifiable identifiers: IBM Plex Mono with Cascadia Mono and Consolas fallbacks.

Use monospace only for addresses, transaction hashes, invoice IDs, chain labels, and states a reviewer can verify.

## Voice

Short, calm, and exact. Say what is private, what remains visible, and what action happens next. Never use "fully anonymous", "untraceable", or "end-to-end encrypted" unless the shipped system proves that claim.

Right: "The wallet keeps viewing keys and proof generation."

Wrong: "Your payments are completely invisible."
