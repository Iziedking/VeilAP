# Veil Arena brand profile

## Brand overview

**Brand name:** Veil Arena

**What we do:** Veil Arena runs private deterministic-agent competitions and publishes verifiable results without publishing submitted strategies.

**Who we serve:** Players, agent builders, tournament operators, sponsors, and spectators.

**Unique value:** The competition is public. The winning strategy is not.

Veil Arena sounds like a tournament director who understands the engineering. It is short, competitive, and precise. It explains the next move before exposing technical detail.

We sound like:

- a fair tournament operator;
- a capable agent builder;
- a public evidence register.

We do not sound like:

- a casino;
- a trading terminal;
- a protocol white paper in the main interface;
- an AI-generated sales page.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Private strategy:** Competitors never receive another submitted policy.
2. **Fair execution:** Every agent receives the same committed rules and deterministic match conditions.
3. **Public proof:** Results, commitments, rankings, and signed receipts are visible without exposing strategy rules.
4. **Open competition:** Anyone can give `AGENT.md` to a coding agent, approve the package, and compete.

## Style rules

- Product name in prose: `Veil Arena`.
- Visual wordmark: `VEIL:ARENA`, with the colon in signal orange.
- Never write `VeilAP` or `VEILAP` on new public surfaces.
- Use sentence case in prose and compact uppercase for arena labels.
- Use the Oxford comma.
- Avoid em dashes, ellipses, excessive exclamation points, and emojis.
- Keep paragraphs short and put the action before the explanation.
- Say `strategy`, `agent`, `competition`, `match`, `receipt`, and `sealed`.
- Avoid `betting platform`, `casino`, `guaranteed privacy`, `trustless`, and `unbeatable`.
